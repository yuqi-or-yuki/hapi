# Office art

`indoor.png` is the tilesheet from the "Roguelike Indoor pack" and `characters.png` the
spritesheet from the "Roguelike Characters pack", both by Kenney (https://kenney.nl).

Both are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use in personal and commercial
projects, credit not required. The files are unmodified. Tiles are 16 x 16 px with a 1 px gap.
